Garlic Chicken

Description

Juicy garlic butter chicken cooked in 20 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 Chicken breasts
  • 2 tbsp Butter
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 tsp Paprika

Steps

  1. Season chicken with salt, pepper, paprika
  2. Melt butter in pan, add garlic
  3. Cook chicken 6 mins per side
  4. Serve hot with rice or veggies
