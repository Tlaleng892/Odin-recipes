Lasagna Recipe

Home

Description

Classic cheesy lasagna that melts in your mouth

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Cook noodles and brown the beef
  2. Layer sauce, noodles, beef, and cheese
  3. Repeat layers
  4. Bake at 190°C for 30 minutes
