Lasagna

Description

A delicious classic lasagna with meat sauce and cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 onion chopped
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Cook the ground beef and onion
  2. Add tomato sauce and simmer
  3. Boil lasagna noodles
  4. Layer noodles, sauce, and cheese in a pan
  5. Bake at 180°C for 30 minutes
